1. Introduction

Welcome to Readora – PDF Reader.

Readora is a free, offline PDF reader application designed to help users open, read, search, zoom, print, and share PDF documents conveniently.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

2. Information Collection

Readora does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location information
  • Device identifiers for tracking
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • PDF documents or their contents
  • Financial or payment information

No account registration or login is required.

3. Offline Functionality

Readora is designed to work completely offline.

All PDF reading and document processing operations are performed locally on your device.

Your documents are not uploaded to any server or cloud storage.

4. File Access and Permissions

Readora may request access to PDF documents selected by the user.

This access is used exclusively to open, display, search, and manage PDF documents within the application.

Readora does not access unrelated personal files or transmit document contents to external servers.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer personal information to third parties.

When users choose to share or print a PDF, the document is handled by the application or service selected by the user through their device.

6. Third-Party Services

Readora does not use advertising networks, user-tracking services, or third-party analytics services.

The application does not require cloud services to function.

7. Data Storage and Security

PDF documents remain on the user's device.

Readora does not maintain external databases containing user documents or personal information.

Users retain control over their files.

8. Children's Privacy

Readora does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

The application is designed for general PDF reading purposes.

9. Free Application

Readora is provided free of charge.

No subscription, registration, or payment is required to use its PDF reading features.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
